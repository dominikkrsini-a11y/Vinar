# Outreach templates (DM / WhatsApp)

Use with warm contacts first. Personalize with their name and grape/region if you know it.  
Replace `[link]` with your real App Store / Play Store / TestFlight / Expo link.

---

## A) First check-in (already has the link)

**HR:**

```
Bok [ime], jesi li uspio otvoriti Vinara i upisati prvo vino?
Ako zapne bilo što (prijava, upis u dnevnik), javi — popravit ću odmah.
Cilj je da ti u podrumu ide brzo, ne da gubiš vrijeme na app.
```

**EN:**

```
Hey [name], did you manage to open Vinar and add your first wine?
If anything sticks (login, logbook entry), tell me — I’ll fix it right away.
Goal is speed in the cellar, not spending time on the app.
```

---

## B) First-success push (installed, no entry yet)

**HR:**

```
Brzo pitanje: jesi li već dodao vino i jedan upis (gustoća / temperatura / SO₂)?
To je cijeli “aha” trenutak — kalkulator i asistent kasnije imaju smisla tek s dnevnikom.
Mogu ti proći kroz 2 minute na telefonu ako želiš.
```

**EN:**

```
Quick one: have you added a wine and one logbook entry yet (density / temp / SO₂)?
That’s the real “aha” — the calculator and assistant only click once the logbook exists.
Happy to walk you through it in 2 minutes on the phone if you want.
```

---

## C) Referral ask (after they logged something)

**HR:**

```
Drago mi je da ti ide!
Poznaš li još 2–3 vinara (isti klub / berba / kupnja kvasca) kojima bi brzi dnevnik koristio?
Ako da, pošalji im moj broj ili mi ti njihova imena — neću spamati, samo kratko pitati žele li beta pristup.
```

**EN:**

```
Glad it’s working for you!
Do you know 2–3 other winemakers (same club / harvest / yeast shop) who’d want a fast cellar logbook?
If yes, send them my number or share their names — I won’t spam, just a short beta ask.
```

---

## D) Cold-warm intro (name from a friend)

**HR:**

```
Bok [ime], [zajednički prijatelj] je rekao da radiš vino / imaš podrum.
Pravim Vinara — jednostavan dnevnik + SO₂/ABV kalkulator (+ AI asistent uz tvoja vina).
Tražim ~20 vinara za beta tijekom berbe/vrenja. Želiš link u inbox, bez obaveze?
```

**EN:**

```
Hi [name], [mutual friend] said you’re making wine / keep a cellar.
I’m building Vinar — a simple logbook + SO₂/ABV calculator (+ AI assistant that knows your wines).
Looking for ~20 beta winemakers this harvest/ferment. Want the link in your inbox, no obligation?
```

---

## E) Udruga / club — 15-minute demo offer

**HR:**

```
Bok, vodim kratki beta za Vinara (dnevnik + kalkulatori za male proizvođače).
Mogu doći / javiti se 15 min članovima — besplatno ovaj mjesec.
Zanima li vas termin uz sastanak kluba?
```

**EN:**

```
Hi — I’m running a short Vinar beta (logbook + calculators for small producers).
Happy to do 15 minutes with members — free this month.
Would a slot next to a club meeting work?
```

## F) Supplier / shop intro (free listing → later paid)

**HR:**

```
Bok, u Vinaru (app za male vinare) imamo popis dobavljača koje vinari već traže.
Mogu vas besplatno istaknuti u beti; kasnije razmišljamo o sezonskom istaknutom oglasu.
Želite li kratki poziv da vidimo ima li smisla za vaše kupce?
```

**EN:**

```
Hi — in Vinar (app for small producers) we list suppliers winemakers already look for.
I can feature you free during beta; later we may offer a seasonal featured placement.
Open to a short call to see if it helps your customers?
```
